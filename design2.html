A neighbourhood practice built on time, honesty, and gentle hands.

32th Oral Care is a family and cosmetic dental practice in the heart of Brewster. Dr. Krishna and our team believe good dentistry starts with listening — so we take the time to understand what you need, explain your options in plain language, and recommend only what genuinely helps.

From a child's first checkup to full-mouth reconstruction, we care for every age under one roof. And when something goes wrong, we're here fast — with same-day emergency appointments and telehealth consultations so you're never left in pain.
