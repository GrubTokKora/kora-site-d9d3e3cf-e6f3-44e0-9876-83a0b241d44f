What to expect at each visit

Preventive

General & Preventive

Every healthy smile starts with a solid foundation. Our routine exams use digital X-rays to catch concerns early, and our gentle cleanings remove build-up that brushing alone cannot reach — keeping your teeth and gums in their best shape year-round.

Cosmetic

Cosmetic Dentistry

A confident smile can change how you feel every day. Dr. Krishna takes a conservative approach to cosmetic work — enhancing what you have with natural-looking results rather than dramatic alterations, so your smile still looks like yours.

Restorative

Restorative Dentistry

When a tooth is damaged or decayed, precise restorative care brings back both function and comfort. We use durable materials chosen to last, so you can eat, speak and smile without thinking twice.

Implants

Dental Implants

Missing teeth affect more than appearance — they change how you chew and how your remaining teeth align. Implant solutions at 32th Oral Care are stable, natural-feeling replacements designed to blend seamlessly with your existing smile.

Urgent care

Emergency Dentistry

Dental pain shouldn't wait. We offer same-day relief for sudden toothaches, broken teeth, and lost fillings or crowns. If something goes wrong, call us first — we will do our best to see you the same day.

Family

Children & Family

From a child's very first visit to ongoing care for grandparents, our practice is built for every generation. We take time to make younger patients feel at ease, and we tailor our approach to each patient's needs and comfort level.
