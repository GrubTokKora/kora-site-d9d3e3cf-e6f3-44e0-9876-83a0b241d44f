"A warm, modern practice where every patient — from first visit to senior smiles — feels genuinely cared for."
Dr. Balu M. Krishna, DDS, MDS — 32th Oral Care

32th Oral Care — formerly Brewster Family Dental — is a modern family and cosmetic practice led by Dr. Balu M. Krishna, DDS, MDS. We believe dentistry works best when it feels unhurried: when you understand every step, when the chair is comfortable, and when the person beside you genuinely knows your name.

From routine check-ups and cleanings to dental implants and same-day emergency care, we bring the same precision and warmth to every appointment. Serving Brewster and the wider Putnam County community, we welcome patients of every age.
