About 32th Oral Care

Neighborhood dentistry, done the caring way.

We're a family and cosmetic dental practice in Brewster, led by Dr. Balu M. Krishna, DDS, MDS. Our approach is simple: gentle, unhurried visits, honest recommendations, and care for every member of your family — from a child's first checkup to full-mouth restoration.

When something goes wrong, we're here fast — with same-day emergency appointments and telehealth consultations available so you're never left in pain or in the dark.
